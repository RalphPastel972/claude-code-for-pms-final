Rules behind each version

Today (release 4.2)

  • 60 seconds to answer a ping
  • Running out of time costs the same as a "no"
  • A responder's place never recovers on its own
  • The handler is never warned
  • Nothing a handler does changes a responder's place

With the fix (all to decide)

  • 90 seconds to answer a ping
  • Running out of time costs half a "no"
  • Places drift halfway back to the middle in about 2 weeks
  • Handler warned after 3 quiet days while available
  • Handler can put a responder back in the queue once; it is logged
  • The four quiet responders are reset once
Sources: Rook database (pings and callouts, 29 Jun to 6 Sep 2026), routing code, Kip's interview of 4 Sep. Weekly pings and dates are real. The callouts on the phone are real Eastgate callouts from early September, reused as examples. Countdowns run 10 times faster than real time.
