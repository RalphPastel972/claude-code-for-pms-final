# 03 · Rewind — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly.

Last session you read four conversations and every support ticket
since 4.2 — and found the two piles did not agree.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

### 1.
show me a graph highlighting the increase of missed pings.
My goal is to understand when i happens, to check if it happens to everyone, and maybe to notice another trend or phenomena we might have missed.

### 2.
Do you confirm those 3 facts :

* After the release, users missed more pings
* After the release, users received fewer pings
* 4 users (Vesper, Farlight, The Undertow and Meteor Mite) are significantly more affected than other heroes

### 3.
can you cross the data above with the tickets we have been analysing before and see if that's a match or if there are discrepancies we should look into?

### 4.

### 5.
based on the data, do we have any clue why 4 respondent are way more affected than other? did they respond to more pings than the other respondents before the new release?

### 6.
